# jev-garden — CTO Brief

## One-paragraph value statement

jev-garden is the fleet's attempt at a self-improving judge: a JEV that grows
from the judgment traffic it actually serves (a non-collapsing field as
memory, tiny deterministic heads as skill), compiles itself into versioned
byte-reproducible weave artifacts in idle time, and serves over the standard
TypeSafe systemone wire so any lane can swap it in with zero protocol change —
escalating uncertain judgments to the hosted teacher and growing from every
answer, under a strict pre-registration regime (22-version append-only seal
history) where every honest failure is a receipted verdict.

## What it does & for whom

For quilt lanes: local receipt-chain validation (next-opcode prediction and
impact-sensitive corruption flagging over real qcells ledgers — 16 ledgers,
1418 rows), served at ~0.056 ms/judgment with escalation to the hosted JEV
below 0.55 confidence under a receipted budget cap. For the fleet's research
program: the first measured answer to the founder's "kinda both" question —
the bake-off priced the parametric tissue (winner on next-receipt prediction),
the qthe integer substrate (real signal), the pure-memory control, and three
ensemble forms (honestly failed) on identical sealed soil. For auditors:
byte-identical JS/Python twins and hash-chained receipts make every claim
re-runnable.

## Maturity assessment

**Working on its own soil, with an honest saturated-soil caveat** — evidence:

- All packaged verification green on this tree (wave-69): smoke 9/9,
  selftest 30/30, bake-off reproduces its verdicts of record (P-G1/G3/G4
  PASS, P-G2 FAIL-of-record), current twin law P-W2a PASS (9960B == 9960B).
- The A-line matured serve under sealed addenda (A7–A13), each with
  registration → results commit pairs and predecessor regression gates.
- Honest limits: the ensemble claims failed on saturated soil (the heads hit
  ~97% top-1 — little room for memory priors), one stale experiment (e_g5)
  no longer reproduces its historical PASS because the twin evolved (e_w2 is
  the current contract), and the CI push trigger is broken
  (`branches: ain]`), so the cloud battery runs only on manual dispatch.
- Deployment surface: serving is a library call today, not a hosted endpoint;
  lanes integrate by importing `src/serve.mjs`.

## Cost profile

Effectively zero: zero npm dependencies, no services, no database; compute is
laptop-class milliseconds (compile < 1 s, serve < 0.1 ms/judgment). Metered
cost is the teacher channel only — receipted per call (P-G6: 589+21 tokens,
283 ms), capped by A13 at 32 escalations per production walk, with each
registration carrying its own spend-cap header. CI is GitHub-hosted runners;
free-tier posture throughout.

## Strategic options

- **Invest** (recommended): it is the fleet's only living-model substrate and
  the natural consumer of the atlas/holes work; the highest-leverage moves
  are a second judgment family (break saturation), automating the
  codespace idle-compile lane, and re-registering or retiring e_g5.
- **Maintain**: cheap — run the batteries per lane, fix the CI trigger,
  keep the seal history appending.
- **Harvest-learnings**: the pre-registration regime, the promotion gate, and
  the escalation-as-growth pattern port to any continuously-learning system;
  the DESIGN.md "registered readings" section is a model for interpreting
  founder directives honestly.
- **Retire**: not indicated — its receipts and its served wire are load-
  bearing for the JEV family's credibility; retirement would orphan the
  A-line's accumulated laws.
