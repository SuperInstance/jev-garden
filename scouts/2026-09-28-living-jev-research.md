## Synthesis for the garden design

1. **HDC/VSA is the honest answer to "not sure vector embedding or torch
   are exactly right"**: both orthodoxies assume gradient training over
   dense vectors; HDC fields (and QTHE as its 8-bit quilt-native cousin)
   grow representations by accumulation — exactly what a receipt-fed
   living model can do at quilt tempo. (Priced empirically: qthe arm
   carried real signal — P-G1(b) PASS — but lost to the hashed head —
   the honest bake-off verdict.)
2. **TTT/TTA legitimizes idle-time compilation** as a training regime,
   not a compromise. The weave artifact = the compiled adaptation.
3. **Escalation distillation is the practical living loop**: judge locally
   (0.056ms), escalate the uncertain (283ms, receipted), compile at idle.
   The garden is that loop, owned end-to-end.
